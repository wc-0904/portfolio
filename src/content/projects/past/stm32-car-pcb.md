A 2-layer PCB for an STM32-based embedded car (18-349). I captured the full
schematic and integrated an **L298 H-bridge** motor driver, **LM1084** voltage
regulators, servo headers, and encoder connectors. I placed and routed the SMD and
PTH components, passed ERC and DRC with zero errors, and generated Gerber files for
fabrication. Separate GND and +5V copper pours handle power distribution.
