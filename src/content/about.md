I am an Electrical and Computer Engineering student at Carnegie Mellon, drawn to
the layer where software meets hardware. I like understanding a system all the way
down: how a scheduler hands off a thread, how bits move across a bus, how a few
lines of firmware turn into motion in the real world.

Most of my projects start from a simple itch to know how something actually works,
and end somewhere I did not expect. I care about clean abstractions, tight feedback
loops, and code that someone else can read six months later.

When I am not debugging a logic analyzer trace, you can usually find me reading
about computer architecture or tinkering with whatever board arrived in the mail
this week.
